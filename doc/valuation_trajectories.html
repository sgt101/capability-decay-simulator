Valuation Trajectory Bands

w(E) = ρ(E−θ)/(1−θ)

Each band is a valuation range, not a confidence interval. Its edges are the two stated value pools for one source — $400bn and $1.2Trn — so the band's width says how much the trajectory depends on which valuation you accept, and carries no probability. Replicate scatter is a different quantity and is reported as a standard error in the table and the LaTeX summary rather than drawn as the same kind of interval.

Rows are the three measured Strömberg learning penalties; columns are the three Dell'Acqua capability scenarios. Within a panel each curve is the median over the six AI levels λ: λ is a dial being scanned, whereas the penalties are three specific empirical findings, so λ is the one collapsed.

ρ never enters the state update — it weights the output only — so every value here comes from one pass of the simulator per configuration, not one per ρ.

Capability against its no-AI baseline, over three careers

All panels share one vertical scale, so a panel that stays near zero and one that falls away are directly comparable. Zero is the no-AI counterfactual at the same tick — a paired comparison, never a measurement against t = 0.
