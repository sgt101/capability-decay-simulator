Capability Weight Grid

w(E) = ρ(E−θ)/(1−θ)

What these numbers are

One person at the top of the distribution, counted in threshold-expert equivalents. engine.js converts expertise into capability with w(E) = ρ(E−θ)/(1−θ), anchored so that w(θ) = 1 and w(1) = ρ. Every capability magnitude the reports carry is a sum of these weights, so every one of them scales with ρ and bends with θ.

Neither constant is measured against anything external. ρ = 1000 is a stated assumption — "one person at the ceiling is worth a thousand who merely qualify". θ = 0.585 was picked as a robustness midpoint of the band [0.30, 0.86], under the legacy calibration; expert_threshold_sensitivity.js and doc/paper.md both record that the argument does not carry over to the world-model regime the reported experiments actually run in. This grid is what that costs at the top of the distribution.

The expertise anchors are measured, not chosen. Percentiles of live expertise at t = 1440 from the experiment.5 world-model configuration at its grid centre, mean of seeds 1–3. Top 1% (AI off) is E = 0.9188.

Reading it

θ is the sharper lever, and it is the one with the weaker provenance. ρ sets the ceiling of the scale and moves w smoothly — it is an exponent base, so doubling ρ multiplies w by a fixed factor. θ moves the exponent, and it collapses: as θ climbs toward the anchor's own expertise the exponent tends to zero and the top of the distribution stops being worth more than the bar it just cleared.

Where w drops below 1 the threshold has been set above the anchor itself — the person counts as a fraction of a threshold expert because they no longer clear the bar. That is not a small premium; it is a different statement, and it only appears for the lower anchors.

The capability sum is a thin tail. At the shipped θ, 44% of people (AI off) or 50% (AI on) sit below the bar and so contribute less than one equivalent each, while one person at the top of the distribution contributes 260. Whatever systemCapability reports is therefore set by the top few percent — exactly the part of the population ρ and θ reprice hardest.
