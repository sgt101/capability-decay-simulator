# Proofreading review

Review date: 6 September 2026.

## Scope and how to use this review

This is a full source-text proofreading and internal-consistency review of `main.tex`, from the title and abstract through the conclusion, including equations, table data, captions and cross-references. Relevant records in `my_library.bib` were also inspected. The manuscript and bibliography have **not** been edited. Locations refer to the source as reviewed; line numbers will move after edits.

The review separates corrections from optional style improvements and questions requiring author confirmation. It is not an external fact-check, a replication of the simulation, or a verification of every cited publication. Source-check requests below identify claims that need checking, not findings that those claims are false. Citation observations are based on the bibliography supplied with this project.

The LaTeX build completed successfully without reported LaTeX or BibTeX warnings or errors. Successful compilation does not establish grammatical, mathematical or bibliographic correctness. Figure artwork and fine visual layout were not separately proofread; the table and caption observations below are based on the LaTeX source.
